Built with clear boundaries

Health education, not medical practice.

KalleMind is intentionally designed as a health-information and healthcare-navigation platform. Its role is to help people become better informed, better prepared and better connected to qualified professionals.

  • General health education and wellness information
  • Educational symptom guidance
  • Appointment preparation tools
  • Professional discovery and verification processes

KalleMind does not

  • Diagnose diseases.
  • Prescribe or recommend prescription medicines.
  • Provide treatment plans.
  • Replace consultations with healthcare professionals.
  • Represent itself as a hospital or medical practice.
